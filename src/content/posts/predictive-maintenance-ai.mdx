# Building an Enterprise-Ready AI Predictive Maintenance Platform

Integrating Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning models into traditional enterprise environments requires more than just making an API call. It demands a rigorous approach to fault tolerance, observability, and system design. 

In this project, I architected a full-stack **Predictive Maintenance Platform** designed to analyze engine telemetry data (RPM, oil pressure, vibration, etc.) and predict potential failures using an AI model hosted on Hugging Face.

---

## The Purpose

The goal of this system is to transition maintenance strategies from reactive to proactive. By continuously monitoring asset health through sensor readings, the application leverages a machine learning inference engine to calculate failure probabilities and recommend actions before catastrophic breakdowns occur.

Crucially, the platform is designed with an "AI-first" architectural mindset. Since external ML inference services can experience latency spikes, rate limits, or outages, the system cannot afford to crash when the AI is unavailable. It is engineered to fail gracefully, maintain high availability, and provide deep visibility into performance metrics.

---
